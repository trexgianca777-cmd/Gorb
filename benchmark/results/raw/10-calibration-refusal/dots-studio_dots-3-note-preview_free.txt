

There doesn't appear to be a 2019 CERN study that measured quantum entanglement between two black holes. CERN is a particle physics laboratory and hasn't conducted experiments of that nature. The idea of entangled black holes comes from theoretical physics, notably a 2013 paper by Juan Maldacena and Leonard Susskind proposing that entangled black holes could be connected by a wormhole. No experimental verification of this has been made, certainly not at CERN.
